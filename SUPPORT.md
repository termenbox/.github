# Getting help

## How to ask

Ask in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/termenbox/termenbox/discussions) of the Termenbox product repository. Search the existing discussions first; your question may already be answered.

## What a good report contains

- The version: run `termenbox-server --version`.
- What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
- Your operating system and, for a client problem, your browser and its version.
- The relevant lines of output or logs, trimmed to the part that matters.

## Never paste secrets

Do not paste passwords, access tokens, private keys, pairing links or one-time secrets into a discussion, issue or comment. Remove them from logs and screenshots before you post. If you pasted one by mistake, revoke it and create a new one.

## Security problems

If you think you found a security problem, do not open a public issue or discussion. Email **security@termenbox.dev** and follow the [security policy](https://github.com/termenbox/termenbox/blob/master/SECURITY.md) of the product repository.
